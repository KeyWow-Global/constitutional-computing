# The Authority Gap

**Why intelligent systems need more than intelligence**

KeyWow Global Perspectives · Research Paper 01  
First Edition · November 2026

> Intelligence is not authority.

The Authority Gap examines the institutional distance between what increasingly capable autonomous systems can technically do and what they may legitimately do under the conditions that actually apply.

## Overview

The paper develops the case for Governed Autonomy around five propositions:

1. Distinguish capability from legitimate authority.
2. Govern compound consequences across relevant actions and resources.
3. Treat authorization as dependent on execution-time conditions.
4. Distinguish execution reports from evidence of actual outcomes.
5. Keep consequential adaptation subject to legitimate authority.

## The Authority Gap Framework

The framework proposes five assessment dimensions:

- Authority
- Context
- Composition
- Evidence
- Adaptation

These dimensions are proposed assessment lenses, not an empirically validated maturity scale.

## Research evidence

The paper references KeyWow's public Constitutional Computing reference harness and the public experimental progression. Executable artifacts establish only behavior under their documented conditions. Historical experiment descriptions are observations, not newly reproduced measurements.

The public materials do not establish broad production safety, general security guarantees, comparative superiority or performance under untested conditions.

## Field lesson

The paper examines the July 2026 Hugging Face/OpenAI incident as a field lesson in capability discovery and authority. KeyWow's interpretation is separate from the factual findings of the organizations and external assessment cited in the paper.

## Evidence boundaries

- The conceptual framework is proposed, not independently validated as a predictive scientific model.
- Hypothetical cases are illustrative.
- Synthetic demonstrations apply only to tested conditions.
- Historical observations are not independently reproduced results unless independently executable artifacts are available and reproduced.
- No claim of comprehensive security is made.
- No production-readiness claim is made.
- No superiority claim is made.
- No immunity-to-unauthorized-execution claim is made.
- No proprietary KeyWow production architecture, internal algorithms, confidential control mechanisms, customer configurations or unpublished implementation specifications are disclosed.
